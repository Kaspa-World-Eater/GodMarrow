# The Second Mouth

Oct 4, 2026 · @Someone

A heresy hidden inside the Bleeding Maiden's faith: where the faithful give to the Mother, the Second Mouth takes her in. Proposed canon; nothing is adopted until you say so.

## What it is

The Second Mouth is a mystery cult of theophagy: its initiates take the god's own flesh into their bodies, and the faithful call it the oldest theft in the world.

- **Where it hides:** inside the Bleeding Maiden's faith, among her penitents, in the same processions and crypts. An initiate keeps the hood, the Words and the cup in daylight. The heresy is worn under the robe.
- **What it takes:** the Mother's flesh where it lies close under the Hide, opened at night in places the Myriad will not go; and the bodies of those she has touched, because flesh she has entered is still hers.
- **What it wants:** to carry her. An initiate who has taken in enough of the Mother stops being a person and becomes a vessel she lives in.
- **Its sign:** a sewn wound under the breastbone. No initiate shows it. Every initiate has one.

## Doctrine

The faithful say the Mother asks to be given to. The Second Mouth says she asks to be carried, and that the faithful have misheard her for a thousand years.

- **Her wound is a door, not a mouth.** The faithful pour into it. The Second Mouth holds that a wound that never closes is held open so something can come *out*, and that what comes out is her, looking for somewhere to go.
- **Worship starves her.** A god is fed by belief, and belief keeps her where she is: under the Hide, in the dark, growing without a mind. Taken into a living body, she has eyes, hands and a voice again.
- **The Words run both ways.** Said forward, they give. Said backward, they take, and the Mother cannot tell who is taking her. The cult's liturgy is the faithful's own litany, reversed syllable by syllable.
- **What the faithful call it:** parasitism. Their word for an initiate is the same word they use for the thing that lives in a wound and keeps it from closing.

## The rite and the grades

The god never enters by the mouth. At initiation a second mouth is cut under the breastbone, opened and sewn shut again with gut thread at every rite, in darkness, to the backward litany.

1. **Vessel.** One mouth. The initiate has carried a portion of her once. Wounds begin to close without a mark; the body runs warm.
2. **Shrine.** Three mouths, the new two cut at the flanks. The initiate no longer bleeds when cut, and dreams in her voice.
3. **Reliquary.** The initiate stops aging. No one below this grade is told what it takes to reach it, and no Reliquary has been seen to leave the dark.

Each healed mouth is a sigil of rank. Initiates recognise one another by the way they hold a hand flat over the breastbone, as if keeping something in.

## The crone

The cult's founder is the first Reliquary: an ancient hag who has carried the Mother longer than any living thing, and no longer ages. Her name is unchosen; she has outlived the one she was given.

- **Her body:** folded nearly double in a chair she has not left in a lifetime, covered in second mouths. Most are sewn shut. A few move on their own and whisper in the Maiden's voice, out of time with each other.
- **Her office:** every initiate is opened by her hand. She cuts the first mouth and says the first backward word into it.
- **Her witchcraft:** she reads the Mother's will in the wounds of the newly cut, the way the Veiled Crone's people read glass. What she sees there she tells no one; she only says which initiates may go on.
- **What she is now:** her own initiates disagree. Some say she is a woman who holds the Mother. Some say she is the Mother, wearing what is left of a woman, and that the hag died long ago in the chair.

## Its enemies

The faithful burn the cult wherever they find a sewn breastbone, and an initiate's bones do not burn clean.

- **The Bleeding Maiden's faithful:** the cult is inside them, and they know it. Every procession is suspected; a penitent who will not open the robe is not trusted with the cup.
- **The Pale Order:** counts the cult's dead twice; the frames of high initiates come up heavier than a body allows.
- **The Maiden's hemomancers:** the faithful's blood-adepts let the Mother in through their own opened veins, freely, with the Words said forward. The cult holds that every hemomancer is a Reliquary who has not been told, and watches them closely.

## Loose ends

- **What did Villa Llaga's Elders keep in their mouths?** The faithful's fourth clause was kept unspoken by every Elder. The cult claims it is the first backward word.
- **Who carved the Maiden a face?** The faithful never finished her face. The cult says she has worn an initiate's face more than once.
- **Is the Mother waking?** Each Reliquary carries more of her. The crone has not spoken in her own voice for an age, and her mouths have begun to answer one another.
