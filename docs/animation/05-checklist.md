# The animation checklist

Every clip and every animated effect is graded against these lines, in writing, in its pass log, like the art
checklist (`docs/MASTER_RULES.md` section 4). A line that fails is the next pass's work, worst first.

**Before the first frame:**
1. **Why:** what it is thinking, what it is made of, how heavy, what the player must read (`02-method.md` section 1).
2. **Thumbnails** of the key poses, in silhouette, from the side and the front.
3. **The timing sheet,** every frame with its hold, and the hit-stop marked.

**The clip:**
4. **Timing is weight:** the holds differ by meaning; the weight reads with the colour off at game speed.
5. **Three parts:** the anticipation, the action and the end can be named, with their frames. The anticipation reads;
   the action is short; the end settles.
6. **The lead:** the eyes lead, or the head for the eyeless (the Ossuarch, the eyeless monsters); when struck, the body
   moves first and the head is dragged.
7. **Squash and stretch:** soft parts squash and keep their volume; rigid parts (armour, bone, iron) keep their shape
   and squash at the joints.
8. **No strobing:** a fast part's silhouette in one frame touches or overlaps it in the next (stretch, smear or add a
   frame).
9. **Arcs:** the weapon's tip and the head travel on smooth curves.
10. **Follow-through and overlap:** at the end of the action at least two parts are still moving at different rates;
    the drag is by weight; nothing stops on the same frame as everything else; the clip overlaps into the next.
11. **Moving holds:** no held pose is dead; something drifts.
12. **No twins:** no frame has both arms or both legs in the same pose.
13. **Secondary action subordinate:** the cloth, plume and lantern move, and never take the eye.
14. **Exaggeration, even:** pushed enough to read at the game's distance, and pushed evenly.

**Seen as the player sees it:**
15. **All eight views** read; the real left views are rendered, not mirrored.
16. **Silhouette:** the action reads filled black on grey in every view.
17. **In the game,** at the game's zoom and light, with the Ossuarch for scale.
18. **In a fight:** it still reads with other things moving ("one idea at a time").
19. **Sound off:** a stranger can say what happened.
20. **Skeptic round:** side by side with the best (Diablo II's, Blasphemous's, Eitr's motion); the worst difference fixed
    first.

**For the world's motion** (`04-the-world-moves.md`): lines 4, 8, 9, 10, 11 and 13 apply to fire, blood, the Red,
cloth, lanterns, objects and spells, with "weight by material" in place of the lead.
