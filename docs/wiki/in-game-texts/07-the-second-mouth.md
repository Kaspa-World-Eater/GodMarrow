# The Second Mouth

*The Second Mouth is the only cult in the world: a heresy hidden inside the processions of the Bleeding Maiden. Its
people write nothing down if they can help it. What is known of it comes from what the processions found when they
burned it out, and from the confessions they took first. Outsiders call its teaching the oldest theft in the world.*

## The door

*Sewn into the lining of a penitent's robe, on a strip of linen, in very small writing. The procession that found it
burned the robe, and the penitent, and kept the strip for the Elder.*

The world is the outside of a body. The Mother is inside.

The processions say she has no mind. She has a mind; it is shut in. Every wound in the world is a door, and she is
trying to come out of every one of them. What comes up out of a wound when it opens is her, looking for somewhere to
go.

Worship starves her. Every Measure poured, every Word said forward, keeps her down under the skin of the world, where
she cannot see or move or speak, and the faithful call that keeping her holy. Taken into a living body, she has eyes
again, and hands, and a voice.

The Words run both ways. Said forward, they give. Said backward, they take. And the Mother cannot tell who is taking
her.

Creation is not finished. It will be finished when she has walked out of the world wearing her people.

## The woman in the chair

*From the confession of a penitent taken by an Elder of the processions before the burning. The Elder wrote it down
in her own hand and sealed it in the wall of her chapter's crypt.*

There is an old woman who sits in a chair in the dark. I do not know where. They take you to her with your eyes
covered, and they bring you back the same way. She has not left the chair in a lifetime; she is folded over nearly
double in it, and she is covered in mouths. Most of them are sewn shut. A few are not, and those whisper all the
time, in the Maiden's voice, but not in time with each other.

She has no name. She had one once, the others say, but she has outlived it.

She cut my first mouth herself. It goes under the breastbone, there, where you can see the stitches. The Mother never
comes in by the mouth you were born with. When the second mouth was open, the old woman leaned down to it and said
the first backward word into it, and I felt it go in.

At every rite since, in the dark, the mouth is opened and then sewn shut again, while the litany is said backward.
After the first mouth your wounds close without a mark, and you run warm. That is the first grade, the vessel. After
three mouths, one under the breastbone and two new ones at the flanks, you no longer bleed at all, and you dream in
her voice. That is the second grade, the shrine. There is a third grade, which they call the reliquary. Those who
reach it stop growing older. Nobody below that grade is told what it takes, and none of them has ever been seen to
leave the dark.

The old woman reads the Mother's will in fresh wounds, the way the Veiled Crone's people read it in glass. She never
explains what she reads. She only says who may go on.

The others do not agree about what she is. Some say she is a woman who holds the Mother. Some say she is the Mother,
wearing what is left of a woman. She is not the Veiled Crone; nobody who has seen her would say so.

They say other things, which I will tell you because you will burn me anyway. They say the fourth clause of the
Rule, the one scraped off and kept in the Elder's mouth, is the first backward word. They say the Maiden has worn an
initiate's face more than once. They say every one of you who bleeds and says the Words forward and lets the Mother
through is a reliquary who has not been told.

Lately, they say, the old woman's mouths have begun to answer one another.

## The walking

*The last lines of the backward litany, as an initiate gave them under questioning. The Elder who took them down would
not write them backward, and wrote them forward instead.*

When enough of us carry her, the Mother will walk out of the world in us: many bodies, and one will.

The faithful call that the end of the world. We call it the beginning, finishing.

*The Elder's note under it:* We burn them wherever we find a sewn breastbone. Their bones do not burn clean. A penitent
who will not open her robe is not trusted with the cup. The Pale Order, which takes our dead for laying under the old
terms, counts the cult's dead twice, because the frames of the high ones come up heavier than any body should weigh,
and the brothers will not say what that means.
