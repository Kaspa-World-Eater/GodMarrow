# The lands of Act I

*Written 2026-10-08 at Derek's word: "create area lore for the act 1 zones so you have inspiration for making each
area too". One page per zone. Read the zone's page before building anything in it (MASTER_RULES section 1).*

Act I is **the Hide**, the dead god's skin. It begins at the edge of the world, on black sand by the Red Water, a sea
of blood. Its first lands are mostly the ordinary world, with the god showing only as a hint; the deeper you go, the
more of the body shows.

**World-building only** (Derek, 2026-10-08): "we're not writing story or quests or anything. We're just building the
world." No page holds a quest, a story beat or a hook to a boss.

## What each page holds

1. **A header line:**
   - the zone id;
   - outdoors or under;
   - the creatures' levels;
   - the borders;
   - what is in the game now.
2. **What the place is**, in one out-of-world paragraph:
   - its part in the act and the feeling it should give;
   - the part of the god's body it is;
   - the one image that sums it up.
3. **Found in the world.** Two to four in-game texts, in the world's own voices (`../../in-game-texts/README.md`):
   - each has a title and a one-line provenance;
   - each tells its story in plain, running prose, as a book does: no lists, bullets or headings inside a found
     text.
   
   The myths live in the bible (`../`). A zone page holds the place's own texts: a local legend, a warning, an
   inscription, what the people nearby believe. No little towns or villages are named, and the Silence is never "the
   Hush".
4. **For building it:** plain design notes for whoever makes the place.
   - **The land:** ground, water, plants and ecology, true to scale.
   - **Light and weather:** by material, never a layer.
   - **Landmarks:** each told by cause, after the pit of offering's method: shape, story by cause, its own material,
     one hidden-source light, age.
   - **The god showing through:** how much, and where.
   - **Creatures:** the game's mix for the zone, and what they mean here.
   - **Sound.**
   - **Keep out:** what does not belong.
   - **Open questions** for Derek.

## Where the zones lie (as the game links them now)

The old map put the Ashen Moor at the centre. It is a bare camp now, and the Red Shore will replace it as the start;
how the start joins the rest is Derek's to decide. The links as the game has them:

- **The Moor** joins the Hollow Crypt, the Drowned Fen, the Old Barrow, the Sighing Ridge and the Hollow Wood.
- **The dry side:** the Ridge leads to the Burnt Heath and the Gnawed Chapel; the Heath to the Ash Shore and Fern
  Gully; the Gully to the Hunter's Cache.
- **The dead:** the Barrow leads to the Fallen Watchtower. The Crypt leads to the Fallen Monastery, which leads to
  the Bog-Witch's Shack.
- **The wet country:** the Fen leads to the Upper Catacombs, the Drowned Village, the Root Deep and the Pilgrim Road;
  the Village to the Sunken Bog and the Well-Shaft; the Bog to the Shack.
- **The catacombs:** the Upper Catacombs lead to the Lower, the Plague Hospice and the Root Deep; the Lower to the
  Smugglers' Hold.
- **The wood and the deep:** the Wood leads to the Crypt and the Pilgrim Road; the Road to the Tree-Hollow; the Root
  Deep to the Broken Bridge.
