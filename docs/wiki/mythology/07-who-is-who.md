# Who is who

A quick reference to the powers, the faiths and the peoples. Every faith is one people's mask on one aspect of what
cannot be seen whole (`00-the-deep-lore.md`). The fuller detail, with sources, is in `backstage.md` section 2; each
calling has its own page (`../05-class-*.md`).

## The aspects and their masks

| Aspect | What it seems to be | Its old name (said by almost nobody) | The mask people put on it | Its herald | Its altar's name |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Bone** | the frame of the world, which will not lie down, and grows | Oss-Vharoth | the Bearing Mother (the Pale Order) | the Marrow Pontiff | Old Upright |
| **Soul** | the threads that pull lives toward each other and away | Yh'Anuul | the Veiled Crone (her circles) | none yet | not yet named |
| **Flesh** | the warm, growing, mindless stuff of the world | Nol-Shogthuth | the Bleeding Maiden and the Mother (the processions) | not yet named | the Red Mother |
| **Breath** | the last breath going out of the world, and whatever rides it | none; asking is an insult | the small gods (the Myriad, the Shrine Keepers) | the Long Exhale | the Last Breath |
| **The Silence** | the untold: the space between words | Ur-Nihl, usually written as a gap | none: the Gilded Peak looked at it unmasked, and fell | a Silent One | That Which Cannot Be Named |

Every herald is a mantle (`00-the-deep-lore.md`): whoever wears it now. They wake at the gods' altars.

## The faiths

**The Pale Order** (an *order*). Its mask is the Bearing Mother.
- **Its dead:** three funerals. The flesh goes to the high ledges, the bones to a numbered niche, and the dust to the
  wind from the highest peak.
- **Its rites:** the Laying and the Count. Bones are held in two hands; nothing goes into the wall nameless; brothers
  are written by number, never by name. Nine is the Mother's number.
- **Its Rule:** *Bear what is given. Keep what is laid. Let finish what is ready.*
- **Its seat:** the bone-houses of the Ossa.
- **Its factions:** the Ninefold, who close the living's counts early, and the Undercount, who go down into the older
  halls.
- **Its calling:** the Ossuarch, the warden of a bone-house and an office of no one man.

**The Gilded Peak** (a *monastery*, fallen in 1098). It wore no mask, and looked at the Silence itself.
- **Its dead** were gilded and given to the Silence, leaving nothing.
- **Its rites:** the chant of one breath, the bowls carried down at dusk ("Put a name in it"), and gold laid on what
  should be looked at and not seen.
- **Its seat:** a mountain of bone, now a courtyard of warm black glass.
- **Its factions:** the Bowl, who still go down in fours, and the Gilt, who gild themselves living.
- **Its calling:** the Empty Hand, "Of That Which Cannot Be Held". What an Empty Hand strikes leaves glass, dust, or
  nothing.

**The Veiled Crone** (a *circle*). Its mask is an old woman, veiled.
- **Its dead:** at a death, every mirror and still water is covered, so the soul can go through the eye of the loom.
- **Its rites:** the circle of names from dusk to the grey hour, the coat of glass, and the year's cloth knitted at
  midwinter.
- **Its factions:** since the loose winter of 1106, the Covered, who send the dead on, and the Unveiled, who keep the
  dead in glass and carry them.
- **Its calling:** the Hollow Mystic, out of the Unveiled.

**The Bleeding Maiden** (a *procession*). Its mask has two faces: the Maiden, the opening, and the Mother, the growing.
- **Its dead** lie in their own wall, each robe on its peg beside them, and come back, they say, as saints on the
  crypt stair at night.
- **Its rites:** the measured cup poured into a wound once a year with the Words, and the procession going out and coming
  home.
- **Its creed:** blood given is not blood taken; the Mother has no mind.
- **Its factions:** the Measured, and the Open Vein, whose processions go out and do not come home.
- **Its calling:** the Red Penitent, who opens their own veins and lets the Mother through.

**The Myriad**, whom common folk call the Shrine Keepers (a *shrine*). Its masks are the small gods, many and nameless.
- **Its dead** are given to the water.
- **Its rites:** bowing to every small god and never asking its name; purifiers taking wrong breath into themselves and
  sending it out by the fan; the Small Cutting, since the grey year.
- **Its factions:** the Folded, who serve while they have room, and the Horned, purifiers at their end, who walk down
  into the dark.
- **Its calling:** the Shrine Keeper.

**The Second Mouth**, the only cult, hidden inside the processions.
- It teaches that the world is the outside of a body, with the Mother inside trying to come out.
- Its rite is a second mouth cut under the breastbone and the Words said backward. Its grades are the vessel, the shrine
  and the reliquary.
- Its founder, the woman in the chair, has outlived her name.
- The processions burn it wherever they find a sewn breastbone.

## The peoples

**The Tithed** are everyone: the children of the congregation that knelt inside the god.
- **Their ways:** salt on the sill and round the cradle; belts knotted at the back; true names kept behind the teeth; no
  singing on the open ash at night.
- **Their nights:** Kneel-Night, once a year; Low Day at the barrows.
- **Their end:** "somebody always sits up". They have no end of their own.

**The shore folk** lived in the giant trees above the Red Water, at the start of the game, and are gone since 1114.
- **What they believed:** the land is the Fallen One's body, the Red is its blood, and whatever goes into the Red comes
  back.
- **Their dead** were burned the night they died.
- **Their ways:** the stair swept every morning, spirit houses on carved posts, and a teacher's dance before every fight.
- **Their forms:** Thai and Northwest Coast together.

**Those who work the lands** (each lives in its zone, and none has a town):
- the bog folk, who walk the Long Back and say their own people laid it;
- the salt-boilers of the Ash Shore;
- the lantern folk of the Sighing Ridge, who stand still when the wind stops;
- the boatmen of the Drowned Fen;
- the hunters, who take hide, bone and tallow from what comes up out of the god;
- the copyists, who keep the registers of the old road's numbered stones.

**Further off, and later in the acts:**
- **the Last Vigil:** lamp-bearers keeping a watch at the gate of the Descent;
- **the Count-breakers:** lowlanders who unmake the Order's tallies at night;
- **the herald-cults:** each worshipping one power's herald.

## Mantles

Every role in the world is a mantle (`00-the-deep-lore.md`). These are some the world knows:
- the Ossuarch;
- the Marrow Pontiff;
- each herald;
- the Ossuary Matron;
- the Carrion Warden;
- the Tallow-Mother;
- the Starved Crown;
- the Mysterious Stranger, who lays the cards for every pilgrim of every turn;
- the merchant at the end of the world;
- the pilgrim.

Whoever wears one now can be killed. The mantle cannot.
