# Port audit, part 1 of 3: `b_core.js` … `zx_minions32.js` against the Godot 4.7 build

Written 2026-10-01 from `web/triune_desktop/src/*.js` (the reference; later `zz_*` files wrap earlier ones, so the
FINAL behaviour counts) and the GDScript in `core/ entities/ skills/ items/ world/ ui/ fx/ data/`. Files in
`src/_exclude.txt` (`zz_hero_hemomancer_r3/r7`, `zz_zz_king68`) were skipped.

**Status words.** EXACT = ported faithfully (numbers and behaviour match what I could read). DIFFERENT = ported but
numbers/behaviour/look differ (how, with file:line). MISSING = not in Godot. LATER = art data, platform-only or retired,
not needed for Act I parity. SUPERSEDED = a later file replaces this one in the browser itself; the final version is
what should be audited (named). UNVERIFIED = I could not confirm either way in the time given; do not treat as EXACT.

**Two facts that colour the whole table.**
1. The Godot data tables (`data/*.json`) and zones (`data/zones/*_s{777,4242,12345}.json`) were **exported from the
   live browser build** (`tools/export_data.js`, exported 2026-09-30, `meta.source_html = /tmp/ossport/x71.html`).
   So skill text and numbers, monster rows, items, affixes, uniques, zone layouts, props, lights and sprites (the
   atlases in `art/sprites/*.json`) are EXACT *by construction* wherever the GDScript actually reads them. The audit
   below therefore concentrates on the **behaviour written in GDScript**, which is where drift lives.
2. The **Hemomancer / Red Penitent is not in Godot** (no `skills/hemomancer.gd`; `ui/title.gd:22` lists the order
   with `false` = not playable; `docs/HANDOFF.md §5.6` says "Later: the Red Penitent"). Every Hemomancer row below is
   MISSING for that reason and is not repeated in the gap list more than once.

## Top 15 gaps by player-visible impact (this third of the files)

1. **The light map is missing** (y_light21.js, still live in the final build): coloured light poured into ground and walls,
   quantised dithered bands, flames raking brighter than paint, the second raised light on wall faces, flame halos
   (`drawHalos37`), the hero's rim light from the nearest flame (`heroRim37`), and wedge shadows from every flame
   (`figureShadows37`). Godot has only the dark-layer pools (`world/dark_layer.gd`). This is the "lantern glow / lighting"
   gap the user named.
2. **Embers and smoke from every flame**, light shafts in deep places, haze strips (y_light21.js:309-465) → not in Godot;
   `world/atmos.gd`/`weather.gd` are a different, invented atmosphere.
3. **The Hemomancer / Red Penitent class** (h_blood, i_blood_ui, zm_hemo22, and its o_skills14/p_ui14/k_arcana parts) is
   absent; its atlases are exported and waiting.
4. **Title menu**: the final web (zz_title54) floats the choices on the blood with GODMARROW in stepped bronze; Godot's
   `ui/title.gd` is a redesign (stages, cards, order pages, Options/Credits). The bowl backdrop itself is captured EXACT.
5. **Hero equipment is not drawn** (zl_heroes22/zq_hero32 `heroGear`): robe/mail tiers, hood/mask, weapon in hand, gold
   trim are repainted per combination in the web; Godot has one costume per order.
6. **Hour banners and lines** ("DUSK — The Hide bleeds…", zv_time24.js:33-44) missing; the kind-specific half-speed
   bonus (`(1+(hourMul−1)·0.5)`) also missing.
7. **Chill/freeze/shatter and the magic arc** on weapon elements (k_arcana.js:93-183) missing; cold is a flat 0.35 slow in
   `entities/hero.gd:457`; no 0.35 s proc gate.
8. **Maps are not random per New game**: Godot cycles 3 exported layouts per zone (`core/game.gd:19`) where the web
   generates a fresh one each start.

   Not in the web (zc_combat22.js:154-195). Also update radii 26/34 vs the web's 24/40.

    PILLAR (zc_combat22.js:45), so worms tunnel through catacomb floors in Godot.
