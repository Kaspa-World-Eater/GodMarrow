# The wiki's lore pages from before the bible

*Moved here 2026-10-09. These were `docs/wiki/11-codex-voices.md`, `11a-codex-stranger.md`, `12-lore-notes.md`,
`12b-codex-interviews.md` and `16-errant-ways.md`. They are records, not canon and not briefs.*

They were written 2026-09-29 and 30, before the makers' bible (`docs/wiki/mythology/`) and before Derek's rulings of
2026-10-08 and 09. They tell much that is now retired:
- the people as the children of a congregation that knelt inside the god and came out of its mouth;
- years and dates ("a thousand years", 1071, 1106, "the Ashwake reckoning");
- living towns, villages, inns and markets (Ashwake, Villa Llaga, Kettlewick, Coldhearth, Cold Adit);
- salt as a ward and a currency;
- the Myriad's "eight million small gods, one to every stone";
- the Silence called "the Quiet" and "the Unsaying";
- animals in the world;
- the Laden Board, the Errant Ways' archetype names and the Shrine Keeper's old trees.

**What they still hold of use:** voices, images and small true details that can be mined, re-told in the bible's terms
and under its word laws (`docs/wiki/mythology/README.md`). Where anything here disagrees with the bible or the decision
log, the bible and the log win.

**`11-codex-voices.md` and `11a-codex-stranger.md` mirror the game's own Codex** (`data/codex.json`), which still shows
this retired lore to the player. Rewriting the Codex to the bible is open (`docs/HANDOFF.md`, open questions).

Older code docstrings in `tools/` (the moor, the Hollow Wood, the Kneeler, the teeth, the ruins) cite these pages at
their old paths in `docs/wiki/`.
