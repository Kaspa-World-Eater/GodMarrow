# The Shrine Keeper: the design

*2026-10-05. Written before drawing, by the Ossuarch's process. Sources:*
- *Derek's three sheets in this folder (the best is `sheet_58e07eae_3.png`);*
- *`docs/wiki/05-class-shrine-keeper.md`;*
- *the mythology page `docs/wiki/mythology/04-the-other-callings.md` (the Myriad);*
- *Derek's lore bible rulings: callings are never particular characters; gate, paper figure and the Horned replace the old Japanese words; no animals; no fox.*

## Who wears this
A purifier of the Myriad, the breath faith. Purifiers breathe the world's distortion (miasma: breath gone wrong) into
their own lungs and carry it, then breathe it out at whoever made it. Every purifier knows how it ends: the room inside
runs out, the face goes long and hard, the horns come in, and one morning they walk down into the water with
everything they took. This one is still walking the Hide, keeping the shrines the others forgot.

So everything on the figure is about **holding**: things bound, sealed, wrapped, tied shut, carried. And everything
is a little too full.

## From Derek's paintings (the look)
- **The hat:** a wide, shallow straw rain-hat, wider than the shoulders, its brim the silhouette's first read from
  above. Worn low.
- **The veil:** tattered lilac cloth hanging from under the hat over the whole face and down to the chest, torn into
  strips. Only the eyes show: two points of violet, the miasma held behind them. It's a purposeful light, allowed
  under the D2R rule.
- **The armour:** dark plum lamellar plates, small and laced: on the shoulders, the forearms, and two long hanging
  panels at the front of the robe.
- **The ropes:** thick brown rope coiled round and round the chest and waist, knotted; the sacred binding that keeps
  what is held inside.
- **The jars:** round sealed clay jars, three at the hips and three on the back, tied on with rope, each sealed with
  a paper charm. In the game's lore they are **breath-jars**: what a purifier breathed in and could not hold, sealed
  and carried until a shrine can take it.
- **The paper:** white paper streamers folded in zig-zags (the paper figures' cut), hanging from the rope belt.
- **The robe:** long, violet, layered and tattered to the ground, darker and filthier at the hem.
- **The hands:** dark gloves with hooked claws (claws are the calling's base weapon). Hands free; the war-fan is a
  separate weapon layer.
- **The feet:** wrapped, in straw sandals.

## The quiet things (the lore, never said)
- **Eight jars.** Six can be seen, and two are tied inside the robe; the Myriad's number is eight million. Not
  drawn, just in the file's comments.
- **The Horned:** where the veil parts at the brow, two small hard bumps press up under the cloth. The horns are
  coming in. Nobody will notice at game size except the ones who look.
- **The violet stain:** the robe's hem is stained violet higher than the purple of the dye. As Miasma rises in play
  the stain climbs (a code effect, later).

## Colour
Four masses only:
- violet cloth (deep indigo-black to lilac);
- dark plum armour (near-black with a lilac rim light);
- brown rope and clay;
- pale paper, with the two violet eyes.

The light comes from the side, as on the Ossuarch, so the front sits in shadow and the rim catches the edges.

## Motion
Light and quick where the Ossuarch is heavy: the calling survives by not being hit. Human library clips for now.
Later: an idle where the breath shows, a slow rise and fall of the shoulders with a faint violet plume on each
exhale.
