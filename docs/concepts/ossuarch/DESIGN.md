# The Ossuarch: the design

*2026-10-05. Written before any painting, at the Tithe-Hand's depth. Sources: `docs/wiki/05-class-ossuarch.md`
(the order, the Count, §9 Look), Derek's direction of 2026-10-05, and his earlier paintings in this folder (the
favourite is `ossuarch_009202c4_2.png`). The painting serves this page, not the other way round.*

## Derek's must-haves (2026-10-05)
- A tall pointed helm in the manner of the Adeptus Custodes.
- A ghostly pale green plume at its top.
- Black iron armour with bone pieces.
- Numerology and counting worked into everything.
- Far more detail than any figure so far.

## Two places this changes the wiki (flagged for Derek)
- **Iron.** The wiki dropped "iron as a theme". That ruling was about the lore: the mills and the bellows. Black iron
  as the *material* of his plate is Derek's new direction. The iron is never spoken of; it's simply what the plate
  is, with bone fitted over it.
- **The plume.** The rule is "nothing glows" (lanterns and wisps excepted). So the plume is a pale material: very
  light, cold green-white, readable on the dark by its value, never emissive. Its ghostliness comes from how it
  behaves (below), not from light.

## The idea: what makes him an Ossuarch and not a dark knight
Everything on him is either **counted** or **growing**. The Order's work is to count bone and keep it in its place.
His armour is that work worn on a body: every bone on it is laid, named, numbered and accounted for. Except where it
isn't. On his left side the bone has started to grow on its own, and the count on his plate doesn't close.

The player should never read that. They should only feel that he is very orderly, and that something on him is
not.

## Silhouette
- **A column with shoulders**, the tallest hero in the game, about 10% taller than the others.
- **Narrow, then tall.** The pauldrons rise rather than spread. The helm's spike continues the line of the body
  upward, and the plume continues the spike. The mantle hangs straight to the ground. At isometric size he reads as
  one tall vertical with a pale flame of plume at the top.
- **The one break in symmetry:** the left pauldron is heavier, knotted with grown bone.

## The helm
- **The shape:** a tall, narrow, pointed helm of blackened iron, rising to a spike about a third again the height
  of the head, like a lancet arch, in the manner of a Custodes helm but our own.
- **The crest:** nine vertebrae (the Mother's number) climb the back of the spike from the nape to the tip, each a
  real bone fitted to the iron, each cut with a tiny numeral in the Order's letters.
- **The face:** a long faceplate of polished bone, shaped as the face of a skull but smoothed, with only the eye
  sockets and the line of the teeth left dark. It is slightly too long for a man's face. Nobody remarks on it.
- **The brow:** a ring of script inlaid in bone runs round the brow band. It isn't the Order's script. Scholars who
  copy it never get the same number of letters twice.
- **The plume:** a long spill of very fine pale strands, the cold green-white of the sky over the Ossa range at
  first light, bound into the tip of the spike by a bone collar. It is not hair and not feather (no animals). In the
  Order it is said to be the dust of his ossuary's dead, the dust that would not go up to the Pogost. **It drifts up,
  not down:** in every frame the strands rise and stream a little upward and back, never hanging, as if falling
  toward a sky above. That is the dust that never comes down. It's the one quiet impossible thing on him.

## The body: black iron, bone over it, dust in the cracks
- **The iron** is black, worn dull, never shining; its edges rubbed to a dark gunmetal. Pale dust lies in every
  recess and seam ("the lowlands named them for the dust that lies on their plate"). That gives the black plate
  drawn edges and makes him readable on dark ground without any light.
- **The bone** is old: ivory through ochre, crazed with fine cracks, darker in the deep pores and joints. Each bone
  plate is the relic of a brother who went before, and each is cut with a name and a number.
- **The colours, four masses only:** black iron, ivory-ochre bone, the dust-grey mantle and pale dust, and two
  small accents (the cold pale green plume and the warm amber marrow-glass). Nothing else.

## Piece by piece
- **Gorget:** high and close, rising to the helm, ringed with finger bones set upright like a palisade: twenty-seven
  (three nines).
- **Breastplate:** a black iron cuirass with a bone ribcage riveted over it. It has **nine ribs** a side, not a man's
  twelve. The count is closed on his chest. The sternum is one long bone strip; set in it at the heart is a **lancet
  window of old amber marrow-glass**, the Marrow orb's colour. It is a material, not a light: dark honey with a
  darker sediment in it.
- **Pauldrons:** tall, flaring upward, black iron with bone rims, each made of three stacked lames.
  - **Right:** clean, ordered. A shoulder-blade bone is fitted over it like a shield and cut with rows of tally
    notches in nines, each ninth notch slashed across (a closed count).
  - **Left:** the same, but the bone has **grown**. It's knotted and lumpy, has crept over the iron rim, and a small
    spur curls out of it. Nobody carved that. Its tally rows don't close: the last row stops at eight.
- **Vambraces:** iron, with the two forearm bones (radius and ulna) bound along them by cord as splints, notched in
  nines.
- **Gauntlets:** black iron, finger-plates of real finger bones. **The back of the right gauntlet carries the same
  grown bone as the left pauldron**: a small knot and the beginning of a spur.
- **Belt and relics:**
  - a broad iron belt with a bone buckle of nine interlocked vertebrae;
  - from it hangs **the relic bone**, the long bone of a brother, wrapped in grey cloth and anointed dark at the
    joints, which his weapons grow from;
  - a small bone reliquary box;
  - **his frame-cord:** a pale cord of knots wound three times round the belt and hanging to the knee. A brother's
    cord has 206 knots, one for each bone of a whole man. **His has 207.** (Root of 206 is 8, an open count; root of
    207 is 9.) A bone bead marks every ninth knot. The extra knot is the only bead out of step. It's the lore's
    whisper that his plate has more bone on it every year, and it will only ever be seen in a close look.
- **Tassets:** long, to the knee, nine overlapping lames each, iron and bone alternating, the bone lames cut with
  names.
- **Greaves:** black iron. **The kneecaps are real kneecaps** set in iron cups, worn smooth. That's the Kneeling
  Refused: he has never knelt in them.
- **Sabatons:** heavy, pointed, iron, dust caked to the ankle.
- **The mantle:** dust-grey, heavy, long, hanging straight to the ground behind him. It's weathered pale at the hem
  and on the shoulders. On the back, embroidered in bone thread: **the Order's sign, a figure bowed under a bar
  across its shoulders.** The hem is weighted with small finger bones tied at every ninth hand-span.

## Around him (code, later)
- Hanging grit and splinters, and a faint trail of pale dust behind each step. It's never a glow.
- The plume's upward drift is part of the model and every frame.

## Motion
- Unhurried and heavy. He never runs; he advances.
- **Idle:** a slight settle under load, as if the plate were heavier each breath.
- **Cast:** both palms pressed upward, bracing a ceiling.
- **Dodge:** a grinding shoulder-roll.
- He's a human, so the human library clips are right for him. A custom idle (the settle) and cast (the brace) are
  worth writing.

## How it will be made
1. **Paint:** Midjourney, from the prompt below. Its job is the look: mood, materials, light, the face. My shape
   model and the Count's details come from this page, not from the painting.
2. **Model:** shape model on the standard skeleton, tall preset. Every piece above as its own solid. Materials at
   the Tithe-Hand's standard: hue-shifted ramps (black iron: violet-black shadows, cold gunmetal lights; bone:
   umber shadows, ivory-ochre lights; plume: grey-green to near-white), coloured broken outline, warm key light,
   cool rim, contact shadow, built at final size.
3. **The colouring port first:** the sculpt kit's ramp, outline and light rules brought into PixelForge's renderer.
4. **Hand pass** on his key frames.
5. **Shown to Derek** (an animated page) for grading. Nothing else is started until then.
