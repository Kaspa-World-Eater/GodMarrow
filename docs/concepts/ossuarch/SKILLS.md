# The Ossuarch: the calling redone (proposal, 2026-10-05)

*For Derek to mark up before anything is built. Sources:*
- *the class page `docs/wiki/05-class-ossuarch.md` and the new mythology page `docs/wiki/mythology/03`;*
- *today's rulings: charged melee with three tiers (a fourth only from uniques), some spells charge, no cooldowns, power
  gated by cost, melee costs poise, no teleport, darker each difficulty, souls in the lantern, no tutorials.*

## Who he is in play

He is slow, heavy and certain. He never runs; he advances. He fights at arm's length with a weapon grown from the
relic bone of a brother who went before. He asks the dead to stand and they stand, and everything he touches gets
counted.

His three trees are three answers to one question, what is owed to the dead:
- **the Ossuary** stands them up;
- **the Carapace** grows bone out of the world;
- **the Count** takes the living down the stair, one notch at a time.

## His resources
- **Marrow** (the amber orb) pays for spells and curses.
- **Poise** (his footing) pays for melee. He has the most of any calling, and his notables reward staying on his feet.
- **The grit** (the Mantle) is the bone splinters hanging round him. It's his armour, the fuel for his bone spells,
  and the shards his dead are made from. It fills as he fights and kills.

## The Count tree, redone: charged melee with the relic

**How it plays.** Every melee skill is a **technique** of the relic bone, set on a button.
- **Tap** for its quick stroke; taps chain into a string of three, the third heavier.
- **Hold** to charge through **three tiers**. Each tier released is a different stroke, and bone grows out of the
  relic into that tier's weapon.
- **Charging costs poise** as it fills; releasing early is cheaper. He walks slowly while charging, and a roll
  cancels it.
- **Points in a technique** charge it faster, strengthen its tiers, lengthen its reach and add effects at set levels.
- **Uniques** may grant a fourth tier.
- **A setting turns charging off** (Derek, 2026-10-05: some players won't want to charge). With it off, holding the
  button keeps striking, as in D2, and the string itself climbs the tiers: the first blow is the tier-1 stroke, the
  second the tier-2, the third the tier-3, then the string starts again. Every technique and its notches stay
  available; only the way you reach the tiers changes. The game already has the start of this
  (`Settings.hold_heavy`).
- **Three kinds of skill, not one** (Derek, 2026-10-05):
  - **charged** techniques (three tiers);
  - **plain** skills that just strike or cast each time;
  - **held** skills that act continuously while the button is down and grow stronger the longer you hold (a
    channel that builds).

  Each skill is whichever suits it, so the tree mixes all three.

**The numerology:**
- a tier-1 stroke cuts one notch;
- a tier-2 stroke cuts two;
- a tier-3 stroke cuts three, so three full charges close a count of nine.
- The curses read the notches.

## The curses: one kept curse that falls on its own

Derek, 2026-10-05: you choose one curse to keep active, and it falls on its own; the others don't.

- **One kept curse at a time.** He sets the curse he keeps, the way he sets a stance. Only that one falls by itself on
  what his blows count, at no cost. The others still have to be cast.
- **When the kept curse falls** depends on the curse, on the Count's numbers:
  - **Open Count** at the first notch;
  - **The Fewer** at the third;
  - **The Weighing** at the sixth;
  - **The Ninth Stair** at the ninth (once a count closes).
- **Casting is still there for any curse:** aimed, wider when held, paid in Marrow, for reaching what he hasn't struck.
- Each curse's sigil shows over the cursed (a white figure in nine strands) whichever way it fell.

So he chooses a stance for how he fights, a rite for his dead, and a kept curse for what his counting does: three
choices, each one at a time.

## Stances (melee) and rites (his dead): our answer to D2's auras

Derek, 2026-10-05: stances to enhance melee, like auras but weaker than D2's; one or two auras too; minion builds
need theirs, but in our own way.

**Stances** are how he stands to fight:
- **One at a time,** switched on and off like a D2 aura, never on cooldowns.
- **A stance holds back part of his poise** while it is up, so you pay for it in footing.
- **Each one bends his melee, not his numbers in general:** modest, felt in every blow.
- The Count tree gets three:
  - **The Kneeling Refused** (moves here from the charged list): he cannot be shoved, blows against him come back as
    small spurs, and his charge tiers fill slower but hit harder.
  - **The Tallier's Stance:** every third blow of a string cuts an extra notch, and closed counts refill a little poise.
  - **The Pallbearer's Stance:** slow and long; his techniques reach further and stagger more, but he walks slower.

**Rites** are our auras for the dead, done our own way. They are never a circle round him: each rite is borne by one of
his dead, so it has a place in the world and can be lost.
- **The Vigil:** he names one standing skeleton the vigil-bearer. It carries a small bell of bone, and the dead within
  its sound strike harder and stand longer. If it falls, the rite falls until he names another.
- **The Order Chant:** one low line, the step falling on every stress. While it holds, his dead move as one, every
  ninth blow among them landing together. A second voice would be a second will, so only one rite is chanted at a time.
- **The Pale Waymark** (from the Pale Lord): a white stone set in the ground; the dead near it do not fall at once, but
  kneel and rise again once.

Both kinds cost upkeep: stances poise, rites Marrow. One of each can be up at once.

## His light: the bearer

Derek's ruling (2026-10-05): no floating lantern. Each calling starts with its own small lantern-bearer, and a hired
companion later carries a second lantern. The Ossuarch's bearer is one of two, Derek to pick:
- **the Candle-Hand:** a small skeletal hand of a brother, candle stubs on its fingertips, crawling after him on its
  fingers. It must read clearly as his, not as a Tithe-Hand: small, white bone, with flames.
- **the Skull-Lamp:** a brother's skull drifting at his shoulder, a wick burning in its crown, wax run down over the
  brow like tears. Its jaw is bound shut with a cord of 206 knots.

Either one stops when he stops, waits at doorways, and shies back from the dark it can't light. Souls imbued into the
lantern burn in its candles.

## His light and his souls

As the world darkens with each difficulty, his lantern matters. Ossuarch-flavoured soul effects for the lantern:
- **a soul of a brother:** the dead stand within your light a little longer;
- **a counted soul:** each closed count flares the lantern;
- **a Pale soul:** grit hangs thicker in your light.

These are examples only, for when the lantern-soul system is designed.
