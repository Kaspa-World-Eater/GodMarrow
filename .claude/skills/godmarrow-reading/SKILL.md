# The Reading (the cards that make a new pilgrim)

A new pilgrim rises from the cave's pool; speaking to the Stranger on the dais opens the Reading (`ui/reading.gd`,
opened from `world/objects/manager_use.gd`). The calling is face up at the crown; for each other place (the brow, the
heart, the left hand, the right hand, the belly, the feet) three cards are dealt and the player chooses one, which the
deck may still turn reversed; one more is drawn blind under it all, in the Red. The Stranger reads each card in its
place and at the end weaves the spread into one telling; what each card does is summed within the caps into the
pilgrim's fate (`HeroStats.fate`). The last words send the newborn to the black mirror.

Read first: the head comment of `ui/reading.gd` (the whole design, with Derek's words), the decision log's Reading
entries (`docs/wiki/01-rules-and-decisions.md`), `docs/wiki/mythology/07-who-is-who.md` (the twenty-two mantles are
the cards), `00-the-deep-lore.md` (the cards), and the Stranger's voice (below).

## The data pipeline

| File | What it is | How it changes |
|---|---|---|
| `tools/reading/words.py` | The Stranger's words (third pass): `RHYME`, `RHYME_ASIDE`, `PLACE_WHAT`, every card's `(short, sub)` reading for every place upright and reversed, the reversal lines, the bridges between draws, the telling | edit here |
| `tools/reading/spread_data.py` | `PLACES` (the spread's places and their pools) and the templates; reads `words.py` and `data/reading.json`; writes `data/reading_spread.json` | edit here, then run it |
| `data/reading_spread.json` | generated (indent 1, UTF-8, LF) | never by hand: `$PY tools/reading/spread_data.py` |
| `data/reading.json` | the cards, fears, seekings, roads, prices, the `caps` and the `value` scale, each calling's `stars`, the effects (`fx`) per place and way up | edited directly now (see below) |
| `tools/reading/card_fronts.py` | the faces: `art/reading/cards/m_<id>.png` (the mantles, numbered 0 to XXI) and `a_<aspect>.png` (the five callings' cards), 54 x 84, drawn x3 | `$PY tools/reading/card_fronts.py [SHEET.png]`: rewrites every face each run (check `git status art/reading/cards` shows only the faces you meant to change); SHEET is a preview of all of them |
| `tools/reading/card_back.py` | the back: `art/reading/cards/_back.png` | `$PY tools/reading/card_back.py [PREVIEW.png]` |
| `tools/reading/balance_check.py` | N Readings simulated with the real data and draw rules (reversed 32 in 100, the choice blind, the caps) | `$PY tools/reading/balance_check.py [N]` |

**`data/reading.json` is hand-maintained now.** It was last generated by `tools/reading_src.py` (commit ffaff54b)
and has been patched directly since. Rerunning `reading_src.py` would overwrite every later change, and on Windows it
writes in cp1252, not UTF-8. Never run it over `data/reading.json`. Edit it with a small Python patch that keeps its
compact one-line form (this round-trips the file byte for byte):

```python
import json
p = "data/reading.json"
d = json.load(open(p, encoding="utf-8"))
# ... change d ...
open(p, "w", encoding="utf-8", newline="").write(json.dumps(d, ensure_ascii=False, separators=(",", ":")))
```

**Balance:** whenever effects change, run `balance_check.py`. It prints lines given and taken, worth given and taken,
the net worth per Reading (mean, sd, 5% / median / 95%), and how many net up or down. Derek, 2026-10-10: "feels like
we get more positive than negative, should be balanced". The 2026-10-10 balance was a net of -0.03 over 10,000
Readings; keep the mean near zero and say the numbers in the commit.

## The words (the Stranger's voice)

From `words.py`'s head, the decision log and memory "Stranger's voice":
- He speaks through the pilgrim, half to the cards and half to the newborn; he may say "you". Wry, unsettling,
  intimate; he knows more than he says.
- The lore lies under the puns: hide and the Hide, Red and read ("Read. Red. Always red."), mourning and morning,
  lie, count, keep, still and steal and steel.
- Every card has its own reading in every place, with subtext and a second reading under the first; never a literal
  body-part pun; every card has its own reversal line for each part of the body, never a formula.
- No colons anywhere the pilgrim reads; no numbers or dates; "Pale", never "white"; never "the Hush" and not "hush"
  at all; no animals but insects; no food or kitchen framing; "deck", never "drawer"; never the g-a-p word
  (Derek, 2026-10-11).
- His words show in Cormorant Garamond (OFL, `art/fonts/`) in a warm parchment tone, never white.
- Effects are shown by default (`Settings.reading_effects`, an option in the pause menu); off, it plays blind.

## Test modes (env vars read by `ui/reading.gd`)

`GM_READ_ACT` clicks for you every 0.3 s and stops where the mode says:

| Mode | Stops at |
|---|---|
| `all` | never: plays it through and the pilgrim rises |
| `slow` | never, but every line types out in full first |
| `end` | lets him lay the rest, then clicks through the telling |
| `final` | the last words, waiting for the click |
| `esc` | presses Esc with two of three drawn, then clicks through the telling and rises |
| `rhyme` | the rhyme sung, his aside half said |
| `stack` | the crown up, the deck lying squared, before the shuffle |
| `shuffle` | mid-riffle |
| `pick` | three drawn face down |
| `flip` | mid-turn |
| `offer` | a choice, weighing the middle card, the mouse passing over it now and then (the ruffle); at the place `GM_READ_AT` names (0 the brow ... 3 the right hand; default 0) |
| `told` | the first card read in full |
| `type` | the first card's reading half told |
| `bridge` | between the brow and the heart, his look at the cards so far typed |

`GM_READ_FX=0` plays it blind and `=1` shows the effects, whatever the setting says.

Captures (a new game starts in the opening cave; `--at` stands the newborn by the Stranger; `--use=stranger` speaks
to him 1.5 s in):

```bash
G=/c/Users/derek/OneDrive/Desktop/Godot/Godot_v4.7.2-stable_win64_console.exe
SPW=$(cygpath -m "$SP")
GM_READ_ACT=offer GM_READ_AT=3 timeout 120 "$G" --path . -- --new --reading --cls=ossumancer --at=6.5,9.5 --use=stranger \
  --shot="$SPW/rd/offer.png" --shot_t=8 2>&1 | grep -i "script error" -A3 | head -5
GM_READ_ACT=end timeout 160 "$G" --path . -- --new --reading --cls=ossumancer --at=6.5,9.5 --use=stranger \
  --shot="$SPW/rd/after.png" --shot_t=14 2>&1 | grep -i "script error" -A3 | head -5
```

- A mode that runs on (`all`, `end`, `slow`) needs a longer `--shot_t`; a stopping mode holds still, so any later
  time shows the same moment.
- `--shot_n` with `--shot_dt` shows motion: the typing, the ruffle, the shuffle, the flip.
- `--cls` decides the calling at the crown (animancer, miasmancer, monk, ossumancer). Test captures use the Ossuarch.
- The whole opening end to end: the flow test in `godmarrow-smoke-and-perf` (`GM_READ_ACT=all ... --flowtest`).

## Done means

- `spread_data.py` rerun after any change to `words.py` or `spread_data.py`, and the JSON grepped for the change;
- `balance_check.py` after any change to effects;
- the modes you touched captured and looked at (`godmarrow-capture`'s `grid.py` to put them side by side);
- the smoke test and the flow test;
- commit `ui/reading.gd`, `tools/reading/*.py`, `data/reading_spread.json`, `data/reading.json`,
  `art/reading/cards/*` as changed (`godmarrow-safe-commit`). New rulings from Derek about it go in the decision log
  (`godmarrow-docs-and-rulings`).
