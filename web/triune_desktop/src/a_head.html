Godmarrow

The god is dead, and has not finished dying. Its body is the land, and what still lives in its bones is in everything that walks. The faithful call it by a hundred names, so that no silence can unsay them all, and quarrel over the corpse. None of them sent for you. You came anyway.

Controls
Controller
Any standard gamepad works: press a button. Left stick moves · right stick aims (left alone it finds the nearest enemy) · A attacks, picks up, opens · X or RT: right skill (hold) · B rolls · Y drinks · LB/RB cycle the right skill, D-pad ←/→ the left · D-pad ↑ skills, ↓ inventory · View: character · LT: map · Menu: pause. In menus the stick moves a cursor: A clicks, X right-clicks, B backs out.
Touch
Phones and tablets are detected automatically. Tap: left click (walk to that spot, attack, pick up) · touch and hold: right click (drag to aim) · buttons at the right: auto-attack, your skills (tap casts at the nearest enemy, hold makes it your hold skill), drink, roll
Left click
Move (hold to keep walking) · click a monster to strike it with your weapon · click items, chests, lanterns
Shift + left
Attack in place without moving
Right click
Cast your right skill at the cursor (hold to repeat)
Hotkeys
Q W E R T Y U F by default. Hover any skill and press a key to rebind it
Skill icons
Click the icons in the bottom bar to swap left and right skills
V · G
Hollow Mystic: wisp choir · golem orders. Ossuarch: army orders. Hemomancer: the Flesh (mutations, grafts, orders)
Space
Dodge roll toward the cursor
1 – 4
Drink from your belt
I · C · S
Inventory · Character · Skills
A
The Inverted Triune: your tarot web of Arcana cards
Tab · Alt
Automap · Show items on the ground
Esc · M
Close panels · Sound on/off

Skills and attributes can be reset for free. Gold is picked up by walking over it. Die, and your gold stays where you fell. Each new game raises a new map; your character and items are kept in this browser.

Your order is not chosen here. The Mysterious Stranger reads it in the blood: Hemomancer, Ossuarch, Hollow Mystic, Shrine Keeper or the Empty Hand.
