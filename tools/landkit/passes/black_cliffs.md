**Confirmed:**

- They are real form on the 3D road: every column is a prism, every cross-joint a real gap, every step a real step.

**The one true detail:** the Red breaking round the columns' feet, and the blood standing in the hollows of the broken
column tops at the foot, like tide pools.

## The brief

- **The headland** closes the beach on one side: a wall of black basalt columns 20 to 30 yd tall (out of frame), running
  from the bluff out into the Red.
- **Toward the sand** it breaks down into a stair of column stumps, from 4 yd down to knee height.
- **Into the sea** the columns stand in the surf.
- **The columns** are hexagonal and five-sided prisms, a yard across, tightly packed. Each is crossed by joints every
  yard or so, its top flat or gently cupped, its edges chipped.
- **The paint:** basalt near black; lit edges where the key catches them; wet and red-black below the splash line; the
  blood pooled in the cupped tops at the foot; grey-green lichen on the dry high ledges.
- **The carved faces** wait for Derek's word on the opening.

## Pass 1 (2026-10-09), then paused (Derek: "Pause on the ocean. Build the starting area")

**Built** (`tools/landkit3d/cliff3d.py`): a headland of columnar basalt closing the beach, built from an irregular
hexagonal tiling extruded to heights by cause.
- **The face** runs along the game's x, so it looks toward the camera. The first try faced along the shore, so the
  camera saw it edge-on and it read as a heap of hexagonal tops.
- **A short talus** of broken stumps lies at its foot.
- **Columns stand in the surf,** stained red-black below the splash line, with the blood pooled in their cupped tops.
- **Each column is stacked in blocks:** each block shifted, turned, narrowed or widened a little, its corners
  sometimes chipped. Each column leans a degree or two and stands proud or set back. The stumps are broken off at a
  slant, and a few columns have fallen out of the wall.
- **The paint:**
  - the faces in strong planes by their turn to the light, each column a step of its own;
  - rain streaks down every face;
  - a lit arris down each column's front corner;
  - small crusts of grey-green and rust lichen.

**Derek: "Those rocks look like fucking ass ... I would give those an f currently along with everything."** The
rebuild after that note gives the columns structure and rhythm. They are still too clean and regular, read as gray
plastic, and the talus is a row of tidy hexagonal stubs. **Grade: D+.** Paused here by Derek's word.
