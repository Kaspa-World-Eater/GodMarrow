# The Bleached Dune by the form law (tools/art_study/dune_form.py), a rival to painted_dune.py

Derek (2026-10-07): "the dune tiles look really good, can they be improved? You'd have to show me ... if it's a
significant improvement, then we go with it, otherwise, for sand at least it was fine. So create your own sand tile
and let's compare them."

**Rules check, before pass 1:**
- **Lore:** the Barrens of Ossa are the god's bones laid bare; the sand is bone gone to dust, ivory and chalk, lavender
  in the shade; "whiter than the rest, smooth as a brow".
- **Real thing:**
  - transverse dunes: a gentle stoss of about 10 degrees, a sharp brink, a slip face at the angle of repose (about 34
    degrees) with grainflow tongues;
  - wind ripples a few cm apart (under a pixel here); megaripples a yard apart, a few cm high, coarse grains on their
    crests.
- **Form:** all height, lit by a low sun with cast shadows; no painted lips or troughs.
- **Fair test:** the same camera, field, ramp, vertebra and prints as the old dune.

**Passes:**
1. The dune, megaripples, tongues, crest grains, low sun; streamers off the brink, saltation. **Graded C:**
   - the structure is truer;
   - the slip face reads as a flat lavender wall;
   - the vertebra stood up like a stick on the slip face;
   - too few ripples.
2. The vertebra moved onto the stoss; broad grainflow lobes with toes at the foot; ripples over most of the stoss.
   **Graded C+:** a real dune. Shown to Derek beside the old.

**Checklist (reminder, waiting on Derek's verdict):**
1. **Brief:** yes.
2. **Scale:** a yard a tile.
3. **Form:** yes.
4. **Light:** a low sun and sky fill.
5. **Values:** two big groups (lit stoss, shaded slip face). The slip face is too uniform.
6. **Ramps:** the shared bone dust.
7. **Paint:** the dither narrow at tone edges only.
8. **Contact:** the vertebra seated.
9. **Detail where it counts:** **fails.** The ripples are fainter than the old dune's.
10. **Life:** streamers and hops, periodic.
11. **Seen as the player sees it:** the game camera, but no Ossuarch for scale.
12. **Skeptic round:** beside the old dune, which is the richer open ground.

**My verdict to Derek:** keep the old dune for open sand; use this as the dune landform placed within it.
