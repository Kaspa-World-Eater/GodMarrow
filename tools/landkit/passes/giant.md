# The towering tree (giant.py): pass log

One piece in work; ten graded passes before it is shown or placed (docs/PAINTED_STANDARD.md).

1. First render. FAIL: a straight tube (primitive stand-in), weak form light, roots bright green starfish spikes,
   the cut top showing.
2. Taper and lean (row warp of the ray-cast picture), a clear lit third and terminator with bounce on the far edge,
   top hidden, big bark ridges, thicker lower roots, darker moss. Better. FAIL: roots still green tentacles (the
   bole's north-moss rule paints them), splayed straight; big bark ridges barely read.
3. Roots: bark-coloured with moss only in patches on their backs; a steady bend each; broader where they leave the
   buttress; blunt ends diving into the floor.
4. Rebuilt from the Hollow Wood's lore (read for inspiration per MASTER_RULES): pale vein-wood skin, smooth; raised
   veins branching up the bole (bruised warm cords, lit edge, thin shadow); roots long downhill toward the Root Deep
   and bending that way, short uphill; dark damp at the foot instead of climbing moss. Pale reads on the lit side and
   the roots. FAIL (worst): the veins branch so often and so wide the bole reads dark violet-red, not pale; a hard
   ring where the flare's normals switch to the bole's; canopy darkening too heavy low down.
5. Four or five distinct cords, rarely branching, narrower; the flare's normals blended into the bole's through the
   collar; canopy darkening eased.
   Graded: collar ring gone, veins distinct. FAIL (worst): most of the bole in near-black core shade (floor 0.16) so
   a pale tree reads dark; veins now hairlines, not cords; root moss still green camo.
6. A pale value structure (shade floor 0.34: moonlit pale bark keeps its grey from sky and bounce), cords widened,
   root moss reduced to a dark trace low in the creases.
   Graded: the lower bole reads as a pale vein-tree. FAIL (worst): the upper bole a flat violet band, nothing to read.
7. Limb scars as beech has them (dark oval, raised lip lit above, chevron brow), two to four per trunk on the seen
   side between 4.5 and 15 yd: eyes on the god's veins; faint stretch wrinkles across the skin.
   Graded: scars present but 2-3 px, read as specks.
8. Scars at true limb-scar size (0.2-0.3 yd), darker eye, stronger lip and brow.
   Graded: scars read as eyes on the god's veins (right for the Hollow Wood). FAIL: chevron brows wide and wire-thin.
9. Brows short (1.7 widths), thicker, softer.
   Graded: brows read. Shown in the game next (checklist 11).
10. In the game with the Ossuarch at the new camera (1.06): the pale vein-trunks tower out of frame, the canopy shade
    lies under them. FAIL (worst): a tree whose foot is far below the screen runs its upper bole up into view and its
    cut top shows mid-frame; too many ghost pillars from trunks south of the pilgrim; jagged steps from the warp.
11. The bole darkens into the canopy's shade and dissolves through the dither from 9 to 15 yd: no top ever seen, no
    far trunk across the frame.
   Graded: no top shows; far trunks fade before they cross the frame; at dusk a hall of towering pale trunks with room
   between. FAIL (worst): the surface roots, long straight flat grey blades lying on the litter (contact rule 13).
12. Roots shorter (1.6-2.8 yd), lower, more curved; darker toward the ground; litter drifted over their flanks and
    taking back their ends.
