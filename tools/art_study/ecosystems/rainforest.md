# Rainforest

*Zones: the Weeping Mangroves (Act III, Shog-Mire), and the dripping gullies of the Hide (Fern Gully at its
wettest).*

## The real thing

### Temperate rainforest
Like the Hoh, or coastal British Columbia.
- **Weather:** rain most days, and mist. Everything is wet.
- **Moss and fern on everything:** trunks, branches (moss hangs in curtains), logs, stones.
- **Nurse logs everywhere,** with trees on stilt roots standing in rows.
- **Huge trees and enormous dead wood,** its decay held back by the wet.
- **Light:** green-grey and diffuse.

### Mangroves, the tropical cousin
Trees stand in tidal water on arching prop roots, a tangle of roots and mud over black water.

## In Godmarrow
- **The Weeping Mangroves** are a fen of the god's flesh.
  - The trees stand on prop roots in blood-dark water that pulses: the heartbeat mud clutches every 1.7 seconds.
  - Their bark weeps resin and something like tears.
  - Moss gives way to a pink-grey slick that breathes.
- **The Hide's wet gullies** are true rainforest: moss curtains, and ferns on every branch.

## Species and elements
- Moss-curtained broadleaf giants.
- Hanging moss strands, and ferns growing on the limbs.
- Nurse logs, with trees in rows on stilts.
- Mangroves on prop roots.
- Black water, or blood.
- Pale fungi.
- Weeping bark (Act III).

## Rules
- **Wet is everything.** Moss coverage and fern density rise with the wet map until every surface is green.
- **Nurse logs** carry their trees in lines.
- **In the mangrove,** trees stand only where the water is shallow, prop roots arching from each, open water between.

## Transitions
- **Into fen:** the trees thin and the water opens.
- **Into ordinary wood:** the curtains thin as the ground dries.
