# Deserts

*Zones: the Bleached Barrens of Ossa (Act II): the Bleached Dunes, the Chalk Flats, the Valley of Standing Ribs, the
Dry Oasis, Reliquary Avenue, the Field of Fallen Standards. Also any ash desert on the Hide.*

## The real thing
Deserts are told apart by their ground:

| Kind | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Sand seas** | Dunes shaped by the wind: ripples, slip faces, the stoss (the windward slope) and the lee. |
| **Stony desert** | Desert pavement: pebbles packed by the wind and varnished dark. |
| **Salt and clay flats** | Cracked plates and crust. |
| **Rock desert** | Mesas, and ridges carved by the wind (yardangs). |

The wind makes everything: the ripples, the scour round every obstacle, the tail of sand behind it, and the sand
blowing in streaks off the crests. Life clusters where the water is: oases and dry riverbeds (wadis).

## In Godmarrow
The Ossa is the god's bones laid bare.
- **The ridges:** the long white ridges are its bones.
- **The sand** is bone dust. The Pale Order holds it sacred and gives it to the wind.
- **The sky** is paler there than anywhere on the Hide.

## Species and elements
- **Ground:** bone-dust dunes with ripples and footprints, chalk plates, bone bedrock.
- **Buried bone:** shards and vertebrae half-buried, each with its scour and tail.
- **Marrow seeps,** and the standing ribs.
- **The dead oasis,** with its dry reeds.
- **The pilgrims' leavings:** cups, cairns.

## Rules
- **The wind map makes the ground:** ripples across it, scour upwind of every obstacle, tails downwind, streaks off
  the crests.
- **Bone breaks through** where the dust lies thin.
- **Marrow** seeps where bone cracks.
- **Life** only at the oasis.

## Transitions
- **Dune into flat:** the ripples die and plates appear.
- **Sand over bone:** drifting onto the plates and pooling in their pores.
- **Into the bone-hollow dungeons** (the Marrow Cavity).
