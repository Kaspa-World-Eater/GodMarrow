# Conifer forest

*Zones: the Chime-Foothills and the lower slopes of An-Vhar (Act IV); dark stands on the Hide's high ground. Derek
ruled conifers out for the broadleaf tree study; this chapter is the highland forest, where they belong.*

## The real thing
An evergreen needle-leaf forest of cold, high ground.
- **The canopy** is dark and uniform: spire-shaped trees, each with one leader running to the top.
- **The floor** gets little light in any season. It's a thick mat of needles and moss, and little else.
- **The soil.** Needles make it acid and slow to decay, building a deep, layered, springy mat.
- **Snow and lower branches.** Snow sheds from the spires. Dead lower branches stay on the trunks as grey twig skirts.
- **Windthrow** is common on thin mountain soil: whole stands go down together, their root plates shallow and wide.
- **Beard lichen** hangs from the branches in clean, damp air.
- **Fire** is the great reset.

## In Godmarrow
An-Vhar's heights are the god's breath: the chime-winds. The conifers here:
- lean all one way, wind-scoured;
- are bearded with pale lichen, their bark frosted;
- shrink higher up to krummholz (bent, crawling trees), and give out to glass and stone.

Bells and prayer-flags hang in them where the pilgrims climbed.

## Species and elements
- Spire conifers of every age: dense young thickets, old giants with broken tops.
- Skirts of dead lower branches, and beard lichen.
- A needle mat with moss, and low blueberry-like shrubs.
- Shallow-rooted windthrow in rows.
- Boulders, snow and rime.

## Rules
- **Density** follows altitude and wind: thickest low down and sheltered, thinning and bending with height.
- **Windthrow** lies in swathes across the wind.
- **Snow** lies in the lee.
- **Lichen** grows thickest where the air is wet and clean.
- **The floor** is nearly bare under dense stands, with blueberry and moss where light gets in.

## Transitions
- **Down:** into broadleaf, through a mixed wood.
- **Up:** into krummholz and bare ridge.
- **Into snowfields** (`shaders/snowfield.gdshader`).
