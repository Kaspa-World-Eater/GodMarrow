# Moor and heath

*Zones: the Ashen Moor (the god's cheek, where it struck first), the Burnt Heath, the Sighing Ridge, the Ash Shore
(Act I).*

## The real thing
Open uplands on poor, acid soil.
- **Plants:** heather and grass, bracken on the drier slopes, and few trees (wind-bent, standing alone).
- **Ground:** peat in the wet hollows, rock breaking through.
- **Fire:** heath burns in patches and regrows in a mosaic of ages.
- **Wind** is constant, and everything leans.

## In Godmarrow
The moor is ash lying over the god's cheek.
- The ash is warm, because the flesh beneath is still cooling.
- Black glass forms where a Husk's blood ran into the ash.
- Heather and grass grow up grey through it.
- Landmarks: the Sighing Lantern, the Widow's Stumps, the Broken Kneeler, the Ashwake milestones.

## Species and elements
- **Plants:** heather in cushions, moor grass in tussocks, bracken, wind-bent lone trees.
- **Ground:** ash drifts, black glass, peat hollows holding water.
- **Stone:** rock outcrops and erratics, cairns.

## Rules
- **Ash** lies deepest in the hollows and in the lee of rocks.
- **Heather** grows on the drier slopes; **grass and peat** in the wet.
- **Lone trees** grow only in shelter.
- **Everything leans** with the prevailing wind.

## Transitions
- **Into wood:** the edge thickens with shrubs and young trees.
- **Into fen:** peat and pools spread.
- **Into ridge:** rock rises.

## Objects to craft
- Heather cushion, moor-grass tussock, bracken drift.
- Ash drift, black glass shard, peat pool.
- Rock outcrop, wind-bent tree, cairn.

## Status
Ground sets exist (`moor`, `heath`, `ridge`), but haven't been rebuilt from the ecosystem yet.
