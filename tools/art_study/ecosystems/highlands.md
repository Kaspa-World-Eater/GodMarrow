# Highlands and ice

*Zones: the Frigid Heights of An-Vhar (Act IV): the Chime-Foothills, the Glass Pass, the Prayer-Flag Terraces, the
Field of Lantern-Spires, the Cloud-Shelf, the Wind-Scoured Ridges, the Breath-Caves, the Bell-Hollow.*

## The real thing
Above the trees: alpine meadow, then scree and bare rock, then snow and ice.
- **Wind** strips the ridges and piles snow in the lee: cornices and drifts.
- **Frost** shatters rock into scree.
- **Ice** glazes everything in freezing fog. Rime grows into the wind.
- **Life:** cushion plants and lichen are all that grow.

## In Godmarrow
The heights are the god's breath.
- **The chime-winds** drain poise. Lanterns and spires give shelter from them.
- **Bells** hang everywhere the pilgrims climbed.
- **Glass** forms where the god's breath froze.

## Species and elements
- Rime-crusted rock, scree and snow drifts.
- Glass shards, and passes of glass.
- Cushion plants.
- Prayer-flags, bells, cairns.
- Krummholz (see [conifer forest](conifer_forest.md)).

## Rules
- **The wind map again:** bare where it scours, snow where it drops.
- **Rime** builds on the windward faces.
- **Shelter** (lanterns, spires, rocks) holds both life and snow.

## Transitions
- **Down:** into conifer forest.
- **In:** into the caves (the Breath-Caves).

## Objects to craft
- Rime-crusted rock, scree, drift.
- Glass shard.
- Cushion plant.
- Prayer-flag line, bell, cairn.
