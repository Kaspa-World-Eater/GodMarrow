# Dead forest

*Zones: the Widow's Stumps on the Ashen Moor, the Burnt Heath's old stands, drowned woods in the fen, and blighted
stretches of the Hollow Wood (Act I).*

## The real thing
Forests killed whole, by different causes:

| Cause | What it leaves |
|---|---|
| Fire | Charred spires, the floor gone to ash. |
| Flood | Trees drowned standing in water, silver and bare. |
| Disease or drought | Standing dead, the bark sloughing off. |
| Wind | Everything flattened one way. |

Snags stand for decades. They lose their bark, then their branches, then their tops; then they fall. The floor fills
with light, so grass, fireweed and bramble surge, and the first tree seedlings follow.

## In Godmarrow
- **Why:** the god is dying, so its woods die in patches. The blight takes a stand and leaves it silver.
- **The Widow's Stumps** are the moor's old dead wood, cut or broken, worn by the ash wind.
- **Drowned woods** stand in the fen's black water, their roots gone soft.
- **No animals,** so nothing nests in the snags. The fungi own them: brackets in tiers, the wood crumbling.

## Species and elements
- **Snags at every stage:** barked, barkless, branchless, broken.
- **Stumps,** and fallen trees in every decay class.
- **Surfaces:** silver weathered wood, brackets, lichen crusts.
- **The floor:** grass and bramble in the new light; ash on burnt ground; black water in drowned stands.

## Rules
- **How many stand dead, and where,** depends on the cause:
  - fire kills whole stands and chars them;
  - flood kills what stands below the water line;
  - blight kills in patches.
- **Falls follow the snags' age.**
- **The dead canopy lets the light in,** so the herb layer surges.

## Transitions
- **Into living wood:** the blight's edge, with half-dead crowns and stag-heads.
- **Into burnt ground.**
- **Into open water.**

## Objects to craft
- Snags by stage and cause: blight-silver, charred, drowned.
- Stumps by cause.
- Broken-topped spires.
- The silver weathered-wood material.
