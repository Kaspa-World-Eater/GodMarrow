# The art library: chapters

Derek, 2026-10-07: "make chapters in the art repository so you can go back and reference them later."

Each study is its own chapter, written from the real world (sources noted), with a procedural recipe for every
material, so a piece can be built from the chapter and checked against it. Read the chapters that touch a piece
before starting it (MASTER_RULES section 1). `../STUDY.md` keeps the older rounds (1 to 13); new studies go here.

| Chapter | Covers | Use it for |
|---|---|---|
| [01 Detail and nuance](01-detail-and-nuance.md) | What detail is (history, causes), the three scales, correlated variation, edges; old paving, column stone, teeth, wrought iron, ash | Every piece: the first check |
| [02 Ruins, ash, rock and scree](02-ruins-ash-rock-scree.md) | Famous ruins and how their stone aged; volcanic and burnt ash ground; rock types and how they break; scree and rubble | Ground generators, ruins, rocks, rubble |
| [03 Teeth, sinew, muscle and flesh](03-teeth-sinew-muscle.md) | Tooth anatomy and surfaces, broken teeth; tendon, ligament, fascia, dried sinew; muscle fibre, fascicles, rot | Fangs, bone and sinew, the god's flesh |
| [04 Stone and caves at 5 cm a pixel](04-stone-and-caves.md) | Why small smooth stones pillow-shade; real caves (lava tubes, columnar basalt, breakdown, skylight shafts); geometry vs colour at game scale; how Diablo II, Dead Cells, Blasphemous give stone depth; a recipe and render tests | Paving, rubble, cave walls and floors, the renderer's edge pass |
| [05 Cut wood and stumps](05-cut-wood-and-stumps.md) | Axe felling (notch, back cut, hinge splinters, chop facets, chips, wedges); how stumps weather, check and rot; the vein-tree stump that still bleeds | Stumps, cut logs, any axe-worked wood |
| [06 Old-growth trunks](06-old-growth-trunks.md) | Douglas fir bark (furrows, ridges, cross ridges), taper and butt swell, spiral grain, fluting; trunks as warped columns | Every tree trunk |
| [07 Faces in trees](07-faces-in-trees.md) | Pareidolia, Rackham's trees, the Green Man; a face built from tree parts (scars, woundwood, ridges, hollows), long, asymmetric, read by its darks | Any organic face in wood or stone (the Blind Face itself was struck, 2026-10-07) |
| [08 The serpent's spine and black bog water](08-serpent-spine-and-bog-water.md) | A serpent's vertebrae and ribs from fossils and Titanoboa (many, alike, overlapping, changing slowly); black bog water: still, mirror-dark, duckweed and lilies; how bogs, fens and swamps hold their structures | The Sunken Bog's Back, its water and plants |
| [09 Thai temples and spirit houses](09-thai-temples-and-spirit-houses.md) | A wat's buildings and its roof (tiers, chofa, bai raka, hang hong, naga stairs, sema stones); materials; how temples fall apart in a wet forest (stucco, brick, rotting roofs, roots, lacquer); spirit houses | The first area's destroyed temple shrine, its spirit houses, any Thai building |
